Using Tunes Off the Chain

Tunes Off the Chain is free to use, copy, share and adapt. It is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

In plain language

You may:

  • Copy and share the book and backing tracks.
  • Adapt them, for example by rewriting exercises or building them into your own materials.

You must:

  • Credit the author (see below).
  • Link to the licence.
  • Say if you made changes.

You may not:

  • Use the work for commercial purposes.

Teachers and schools

Teachers and schools may include this material in resources for their students and may charge a fee that covers only the costs of copying, printing or distribution. Selling the work, or including it in a product sold for profit, requires permission from the author. You do not need to ask permission.

How to credit

Cameron Horsburgh, Tunes Off the Chain, tunesoffthechain.au

If you adapted the material, add: “Adapted from the original.”

Third-party material

All backing track recordings are by the author. Some tunes are public domain or used with permission, and not every tune carries the same licence. Each tune’s footer says what applies, and the full list is here: https://tunesoffthechain.au/credits.html
